Data sources
  • Historical wildfires: Canadian National Fire Database (NFDB), Natural Resources Canada / Canadian Forest Service. Fires of 200 ha or larger.
  • Flood events: Natural Resources Canada, Historical Flood Events (HFE), compiled from the Canadian Disaster Database, provincial and media sources. Open Government Licence – Canada.
  • Flooded areas: Natural Resources Canada, Emergency Geomatics Service, Floods in Canada – Archive. Flood extents mapped from satellite imagery (RADARSAT and RADARSAT Constellation Mission) on a best-effort basis, clipped to Saskatchewan. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.
  • Active fires and satellite hotspots: Canadian Wildland Fire Information System (CWFIS), Natural Resources Canada; active fires as reported by Saskatchewan.
  • Fire weather and fire danger: CWFIS, Natural Resources Canada.
  • Highways: Government of Saskatchewan GeoHub (Highways, official site).
  • Provincial border: Statistics Canada boundary file (via geoBoundaries), Statistics Canada Open Licence.
  • Friendship Centre locations and funding: Aboriginal Friendship Centres of Saskatchewan.
  • Basemap: © OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenMapTiles, via OpenFreeMap.
